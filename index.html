✓

Background Check (Risk Status)

Your background check usually starts automatically when you register as an adult volunteer, so you do not need to initiate it separately.

If you receive an email from Sterling or First Advantage, follow the link in that email to complete the requested information. Processing can take up to 10–15 business days.

Check your Risk Status under Sports Connect → Volunteer. Youth referees do not require a background check.

▣

Fingerprinting (Live Scan)

Adult volunteers who need fingerprinting should download and print the Region 56 Live Scan form and bring it to a Live Scan location.

  1. Use the location listed on the form and the charge will go directly to AYSO Region 56.
  2. If you use another Live Scan location, make sure the provider uses the AYSO information on the form so the results are sent to AYSO.
  3. If you pay at another location, email your receipt to treasurer@ayso56.org to request reimbursement.

Once completed, AYSO fingerprinting does not expire.

SafeSport Training

SafeSport is not an AYSOU course. Use the dedicated AYSO SafeSport enrollment link to access training through the U.S. Center for SafeSport.

  1. Open the SafeSport enrollment link below.
  2. Create a SafeSport account or sign in to your existing account.
  3. Complete your assigned SafeSport training or annual refresher.
  4. Make sure your name, date of birth, and email address match your AYSO volunteer record so your completion can update automatically, generally within one day.

SafeSport renews annually. The initial course takes approximately 90 minutes; the next three annual refreshers take approximately 30 minutes each, followed by the full course again.

Online coach-specific training

Find courses: Sports Connect → Volunteer → AYSOU → Training Library → Coaching → View Courses. Select Enroll for your course. Course labels may vary slightly.

  • U5–U6: “6U Coach – Full Online Course” (or “6U Coach Online”). No required in-person session.
  • U7–U8: “8U Coach – Full Online Course” (or “8U Coach Online”). No required in-person session.
  • U9–U10: “10U Coach – Online + In-Person Course.” Complete the online course, then enroll separately in the 10U field session.
  • U11–U12: “12U Coach Training Pre-Course (Part 1)” online, followed by “12U Coach (Part 2)” in person. 10U certification is a prerequisite.
  • U13–U14: “Intermediate 14U Coach Pre-Course (Part 1)” online, followed by the Intermediate in-person course. 12U certification is a prerequisite.
  • U16–U19: “Advanced Coach Pre-Course” online, followed by the Advanced in-person course. Intermediate certification is a prerequisite.

Important: Complete the online pre-course before attending the corresponding in-person class. Find and enroll in in-person classes under AYSOU → Training Event. The Training Finder checklist shows the full prerequisite sequence.

Online referee-specific training

Find courses: Sports Connect → AYSOU → Training Library → Refereeing → View Courses. Select Enroll for the appropriate course.

  • U7–U8: “8U Official – Full Online Course” (or “8U Official Online”). An alternative is the full in-person 8U Official course.
  • U9–U10: Regional Referee certification is required. Choose one of the three training pathways explained below. The Companion pathway is the most common.
  • U11–U12: Regional Referee minimum, plus sufficient experience and Referee Administrator approval.
  • U13–U14: Regional Referee minimum, with Intermediate Referee strongly preferred; experience and Referee Administrator approval required.
  • U16–U19: Intermediate Referee minimum, plus experience and Referee Administrator approval.

Intermediate Referee: Requires Regional certification, game experience, Intermediate training and an observed center-referee assignment. Advanced: Contact the Referee Administrator for current course, assessment and possible fitness requirements.

Regional Referee: choose ONE training pathway

  1. Complete Online: Take the full Regional Referee online course. No in-person class is part of this pathway.
  2. Companion (most common): Enroll in the Regional Referee Companion ONLINE course, then enroll separately in the Regional Referee Companion IN-PERSON session. Both must say Companion. Complete the online portion before attending the in-person session.
  3. Complete In-Person: Enroll in the full Regional Referee in-person course. No separate online Companion course is part of this pathway.

Check before enrolling: Do not mix the full online course with a Companion in-person session. In AYSOU, find online courses under Training Library and in-person sessions under Training Event. Check that your selected course names match your chosen pathway.

For questions about the correct pathway, experience or division eligibility, contact rra@ayso56.org.
